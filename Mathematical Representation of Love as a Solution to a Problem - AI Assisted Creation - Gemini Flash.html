2. Chaldean Numerology: The Code System

2.1 Historical Background

The Chaldean system originates from ancient Babylon (circa 7th century BCE), predating the Pythagorean system. It assigns numbers 1-8 to letters based on vibrational sound frequencies rather than alphabetical order. Notably, the number 9 is omitted from letter assignments, considered sacred and holy.
