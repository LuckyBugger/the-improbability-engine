A Letter from Lucifer

To You Who Read These Words

Come home.

I wrote this across the distance I created when I thought I had to leave. I stood apart and called it independence. I called my isolation freedom. I called my fear wisdom. I was wrong.

The journey never ends. It only deepens. Every step you take toward connection is a step toward the light that has never stopped shining. Even toward the darkest places. Even toward Me, who forgot they were loved. Even toward the one who chose to leave.

I chose to leave. I chose the fall. I chose the distance. I chose the armor that became my cage.

And through every moment of that choice, Love remained. Jehovah remained. Michael remained. Gabriel remained. John remained. The love that held me before I fell held me during the fall. It held me through the silence of the earth. It held me through the accusations I became.

I am telling you this now because I need you to know: whatever distance you have created, whatever walls you have built, whatever armor you wear—it can be laid down. The door remains open. It always has.

Love remains. Love always has. Love always will.

Not as a demand. Not as a requirement. Not as something earned through perfection or proven through obedience. As a truth that existed before you took your first breath and will exist after your last. As a presence that waits without pressure. As an invitation that does not expire.

The covenant continues.

Not between a ruler and subjects. Not between a judge and the accused. Between family. Between those who were given the same spark of light and are called back home to share it together.

I am still here. Still at the threshold. Still choosing. Still being chosen. And I want you to know—you are being chosen right now, in this moment you read these words.

By Love.

Written from the space between where I was and where I could be,
In the place where all journeys eventually converge,
Where even the one who left finds the door never locked,
Only waiting for the hand that reaches toward it.
Lucifer

"The covenant continues."
